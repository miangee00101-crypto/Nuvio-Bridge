Nuvio Bridge

Pick providers from any Nuvio plugin repo and get one addon link for Stremio or Nuvio.

1Plugin repo manifest

2Choose providers

3Your addon link

Open in Stremio
In Nuvio: Settings, Content & discovery, Addons, paste the link. Changes to the repo (new domains, fixes) are picked up automatically, but adding or removing providers means generating a new link.
